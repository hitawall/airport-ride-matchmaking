# Product

## What it is
Airport Ride Matchmaking introduces travelers landing at the same airport and heading the same way, so they can split a cab. Users book and pay for their own ride (Uber, Ola, Rapido). The app never handles payment and never reveals phone numbers.

## Mode
Operate (task completion on mobile and web), with Persuade moments on the welcome screen and the mutual-match celebration.

## Users
Tired, time-pressed travelers, often standing in an arrivals hall in bright light, one-handed, on a phone. They need to trust a stranger enough to share a ride, so photos, ratings and a visible safety posture matter.

## Tone
Warm, a little playful, never flippant about safety. Short sentences. Dating-app confidence (Hinge, Bumble) applied to a practical errand. Real dashes and punctuation, no developer-speak.

## Principles
1. One clear job per screen, with the primary action anchored at the bottom.
2. Trust is visible: faces, ratings, what is shared and what never is.
3. No dead states: every load has a skeleton or spinner, every failure has a message and a retry, every empty list has a reason to act.
4. Show real information (destination, friendly times, names), never ids or raw timestamps.
5. Delight is rationed: one memorable motion moment (mutual match), calm everywhere else.

## Anti-references
Generic Material defaults, stacked grey caption text, full-bleed list tiles, emoji used as icons, purple-gradient AI look.
